---
partOfSpeech: noun
ipa: /tanki/
etymology: tan + ki
definition: Child, pup, kit, little one, kiddo (lit. child person)
relatedWords: Tan, Tankoe
---
General term for those who are older than a toddler, but are prepubescent. When used to address someone, its similar to "kiddo"
