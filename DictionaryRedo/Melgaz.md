Melgaz is the word for both our galaxy, and galaxies conceptually. Mostly used for the galactic disk visible at night, but with the introduction of modern astronomy to Wisland, the term *Ishin Melgaz*, our (inclusive) galaxy, is used to mean the Milky Way in contrast to other galaxies.
