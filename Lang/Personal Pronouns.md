
Wislandic personal pronouns are fairly straight forward, but there is an exclusivity distinction in the first person. Us (inclusive to the listener), vs Us (excluding the listener)


### 1st person

|      | Singular | Plural (in.) | Plural (ex.) |
| :--: | :------: | :----------: | :----------: |
| Nom. |   ish    |     ishi     |      em      |
| Gen. |  ishin   |    ishhe     |     nem      |
| Acc. |   ith    |     ithi     |     them     |
| Dat. |    is    |     isse     |     ems      |
