
known as Wis'skel in Wislandic, it is the native and primary writing system of the continent. While foreign scripts aren't unheard of since major contact with the outlands was established, Wis'skel is what every wislander learns during their primary education

it follows its own "alphabetical order" which roughly moves from the front of the mouth to the back, although it doesn't match perfectly.

There are 4 "villages" or groupings within the alphabet, those of the front, the middle, the back of the mouth, and the silent ones. (they were added most recently, and consists of the geminate marker, and two letters that only exist to transcribe foreign words that have the /v/ or /ð/ sounds, which are pronounced identical to their voiceless counterparts)
