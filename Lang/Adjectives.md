
Adjectives decline with the noun they modify.

Adjectives end in "a" generally (a few exceptions, some nouns play dual roles as both a noun and adjective)

Adjectives are often formed from nouns by nominalizing the genitive singular

Dau - two (number)
Daun - of two/two's
Dauna - adjectival form of Daun


To turn an adjective back into a noun, a specific renominalization suffix will be applied, depending on the meaning

-z standard meaning. normally equivalent to English -ness
-k derived meaning. related to the adjective, but not the same thing
-m TBD

compare:
	Dauna > Daunaz : duality, twinness, "dual nature")
	Duana > Daunak : twin (the twin of another), geminate letter
