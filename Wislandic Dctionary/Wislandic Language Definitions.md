# Wisleidenn
*wisleidenn*
**/wislɛidɛn/**

1. (n.) One of the many epithets/titles given to the Goddess of Wisland, used both to describe Her, and as euphemism for Her sacred name when not directly invoking her presence/attention.
   
   A nominalization of the phrase *wis leiden*, or Lord/Lady of Wis. Only ever used in singular. The generic term for nobility is kept two words and *leid* is declined normally. Despite being written with two "n's", it is not pronounced as such, and the double n is purely do distinguish the word when written from its generic counterpart.
   
| Declension |     Form     |
| :--------: | :----------: |
|    Nom.    |  wisleidenn  |
|    Gen.    | wisleidenna  |
|    Acc.    | wisleidennth |
|    Dat.    | wisleidenns  |
