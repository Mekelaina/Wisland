
Six and One deities reside in wisland. 

There is the the Goddess of Wis (~~Elwaiz~~), who has many titles and whose sacred name can only be spoken by those She deems worthy, for speaking Her name invokes Her presence, draws Her attention. She is a very busy Goddess, and therefore, one must not use it frivolously, lest you invoke her ire or misuse her trust. 

Some common titles, and their Wislandic translation:

Wohhinna (Allmother), Kon Drakki (The Dragon), Wisleidenn (Lady of Wis), Wohwizki (All Wise One), Ithenki (One of the first flame), Misettankoe, (Child of the Sun), Wohfekki (One most high)...

The specific title/epithet used depends on the context of prayers to Her or the person talking about Her. Wohhinna, for example, is often used during prayers or rituals for those struggling to conceive or to ensure bountiful harvests; while Wohwizki might be used when seeking guidance in making important decisions or when seeking knowledge.
